Nila Child Safety Standards

Protecting users from child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

1. About Nila

Nila is an adult social and dating application intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. Nila does not permit minors to use the dating or social functionality of the service.

2. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Nila has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Users must not create, upload, request, possess, share, distribute, promote, or facilitate child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any other content or behavior that sexually exploits or endangers a child.

Prohibited behavior includes, but is not limited to:

  • Sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors.
  • Grooming or attempting to establish contact with a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Requesting, offering, sharing, or distributing sexual images involving minors.
  • Using Nila to facilitate trafficking, exploitation, or abuse of children.
  • Attempting to evade age restrictions to access or contact minors.

3. Reporting inside Nila

Users can report another user, profile, message, image, or other content directly from the Nila app. Reports should include the reason and, where appropriate, additional details that help our safety team investigate.

If you believe content or behavior involves a child or possible CSAE/CSAM, select the child-safety-related reporting reason and provide the relevant details.

4. Blocking

Nila provides a blocking mechanism so users can prevent another account from contacting them or interacting with them. Blocking does not replace reporting when content or conduct may violate Nila's safety rules.

5. How Nila handles safety reports

Nila reviews reports using its safety and enforcement procedures. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, actions may include removing prohibited content, restricting or suspending an account, permanently banning an account, preserving relevant information where legally appropriate, and escalating matters to appropriate authorities.

When Nila obtains actual knowledge of CSAM, Nila will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and these standards, including removal or restriction of the material and account action where appropriate.

6. Child safety laws and reporting to authorities

Nila maintains a process for handling confirmed CSAM and other child-safety matters in accordance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements. Where legally required, confirmed CSAM or relevant information will be reported to the appropriate authority, which may include the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional authority.

Nila may preserve and disclose information when required or permitted by law or valid legal process.

8. Enforcement

Violations of these standards may result in content removal, warnings, feature restrictions, temporary suspension, permanent account termination, and/or reporting to appropriate authorities where required or appropriate.

9. Contact and additional policies

For general support, users should use the support/reporting mechanism available inside the Nila app. Nila's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply to use of the service.

10. Policy updates

Nila may update these Child Safety Standards when its safety procedures, product features, or applicable legal and platform requirements change.
